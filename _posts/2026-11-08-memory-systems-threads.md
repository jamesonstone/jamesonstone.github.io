There are hundreds of parallel threads. They come and go. Sometimes they are parsed together. Almost none of them should be on stage.

A thread is not a ticket. It is a live involvement that can thicken, braid, go quiet, or break, including while one is not facing it. Presence is not owed a plot. A thread can vanish without being finished.

The temptation is to ask whether each thread can be measured in progress, and whether progress stops when one looks away. Measuring hundreds of threads is how this becomes a project tracker. Most threads should not have a score.

Two motions continue without attention, and they are not the same. The world keeps moving: time passes, someone replies, a season arrives. That is not dreaming. Dreaming is a not-conscious pass that walks a thread one is not facing and comes back with a proposal. Incubation, not completion.

If the dream is allowed to update the thread itself, a second self writes truth while one sleeps. If it only proposes, the thread can grow without being conscious, and nothing becomes real until reconcile.
