Threads are closer to weather than to work.

Weather has a reason without having a goal. A front moves because of pressure and temperature, not because a plan is forty percent done. One does not make weather progress. One reads conditions. An overcast thread is still there, and not for this moment. That is not failure.

Project-tracking cannot say this. It needs a status, an owner, a next action. Weather needs none of those in order to be real. It can thicken, meet another front, or go still.

Dreaming, under this analogy, is less "keep working the thread" than watching the pressure change: noticing that two fronts might meet, or that something once climatic has gone quiet. It proposes a shift in conditions, not a next task.
