Applied philosophy as engineering means designing and implementing philosophical ideas in the real world.

A claim about autonomy becomes a distribution of authority. A theory of fairness becomes an allocation rule. Implementation forces decisions an argument can leave conveniently unresolved.

The gap worth closing is between defending an idea and constructing the conditions under which it must operate. When the consequences contradict the intention, the premise belongs on the drawing board alongside the design.

We're the engineers that philosophers aren't.

It's like Schrödinger, with real cats.
