Applied science helps realize an objective. Applied philosophy treats the objective itself as design material.

A premise about how people ought to live becomes an arrangement they actually inhabit. Its abstractions allocate authority; its definitions distribute consequences. Operational success can demonstrate perfect fidelity to a terrible idea. The premise has to answer for what it makes compulsory.

The name [already exists](https://www.appliedphil.org/society-for-applied-philosophy-annual-conference-2018/). The revolution may have to settle for a department.

We're the engineers that philosophers aren't. Want to join?

It's like Schrödinger, with real cats.
