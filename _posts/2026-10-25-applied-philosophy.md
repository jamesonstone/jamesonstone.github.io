[Applied philosophy](https://www.appliedphil.org/society-for-applied-philosophy-annual-conference-2018/) already exists. I want to practice it as engineering.

Applied science helps realize an objective. I'm interested in its legitimacy: the premises, definitions, and values that made it an objective in the first place.

Those are design material. Instantiate them. Let their consequences interrogate them. Operational success can demonstrate perfect fidelity to an indefensible premise.

We're the engineers that philosophers aren't. Want to join?
