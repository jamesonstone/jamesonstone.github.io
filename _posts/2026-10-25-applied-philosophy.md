[Applied philosophy](https://www.appliedphil.org/society-for-applied-philosophy-annual-conference-2018/) already exists. Its engineering task is to make premises answerable to their consequences.

Applied science helps realize an objective. What legitimizes it? The premises, definitions, and values that constitute an objective are design material.

Instantiate them. Let the consequences interrogate them. Operational success can demonstrate perfect fidelity to an indefensible premise.

The engineers that philosophers aren't: accountable for what an idea makes compulsory. Want to join?
