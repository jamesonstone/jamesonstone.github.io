The slide into recommendation is easy, and it is the wrong direction.

Once "use" becomes a score — clicked, cited, taken up — the system is predicting what will be engaged next. That is IntelliSense with a philosophical coat of paint. Recsys asks what one is likely to want. A toward asks what the scene is, then refuses everything else.

The tell is familiar: ranking candidates, optimizing for uptake, promoting what was handled and retiring what was ignored. That is feedback for a recommender. Quiet constraints that shaped the next move without being named would lose. Feelings that arrived unwanted would be optimized away or chased.

Fitness, if it exists later, is whether the toward itself was true — not whether an item got taken up. Chekhov's rule bans decoration. It does not rank props.
