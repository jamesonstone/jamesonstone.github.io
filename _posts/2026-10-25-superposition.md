---

An idea exists in superposition until articulation assigns it state.

AI elaborates that provisional interpretation faster than it can be interrogated. Refining its coherence displaces examining its fidelity.

The influence reaches backward: anticipating the response conditions thought into forms the model can develop.

Premature articulation can foreclose the abstraction that would negate the problem entirely. The first interpretation becomes the only problem available to solve.
