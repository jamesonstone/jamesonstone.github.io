title: "Superposition"
date: 2026-10-25 11:21:00 -0400
---

An idea exists in superposition until articulation assigns it state. Several possible abstractions coexist before language establishes their boundaries and relationships. Explaining the idea privileges one configuration, often before the alternatives have become distinguishable. A provisional choice of words can therefore acquire the authority of a discovery: the explanation appears to reveal what the idea was, although it has participated in determining what the idea becomes. Articulation is necessary for scrutiny, but the precision it produces can exceed the understanding that justified it. An intuition need not contain some pristine truth to deserve that distinction. Its unresolvedness preserves possibilities that an explanation may exclude without ever examining them.

AI accelerates this commitment. A tentative formulation becomes a framework, acquires implications, and generates a program of investigation before its fidelity to the original intuition has been assessed. Coherence supplies its own momentum. Each elaboration can be internally defensible while carrying the argument farther from the possibility that prompted it. Attention shifts toward repairing inconsistencies within the explanation; questioning the explanation's jurisdiction becomes increasingly disruptive. The accumulated reasoning creates an obligation to reconcile subsequent thoughts with a structure that began as an experiment. Cheap elaboration can make reframing expensive. The difficulty is epistemic before it becomes operational: a convincing answer changes which question seems to have been worth asking.

The influence can reach backward into conception itself. Repeated interaction teaches which formulations receive productive responses, and anticipation can promote those formulations into habits of thought. Context, objectives, and decomposable tasks become the grammar in which an intuition must appear before it is considered intelligible. Possibilities that resist that grammar may disappear before a prompt exists. This is the deeper loss represented by superposition: the capacity to discover an abstraction that changes the objective, dissolves a distinction, or negates the problem entirely. Preserving unresolvedness indefinitely would merely protect confusion from criticism. The discipline is to keep articulation provisional, including the assumptions that make its subsequent development seem natural. Otherwise execution can perfect the very frame that a better abstraction would have made unnecessary.
